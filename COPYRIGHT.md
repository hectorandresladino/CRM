## Recommended protection steps

1. Decide whether future releases remain open source or move to a proprietary
   or dual-license model with qualified legal advice.
2. Select a distinctive product name only after clearance searches in each
   target country and relevant Nice classes.
3. Register the software and its substantial new versions with Colombia's
   Dirección Nacional de Derecho de Autor (DNDA).
4. Apply for the word mark and logo with Colombia's Superintendencia de
   Industria y Comercio (SIC), followed by international filings as markets
   justify them.
5. Obtain written copyright assignments or contributor agreements for every
   external contribution, design, illustration and commissioned component.
6. Keep dated releases, signed tags, SBOMs and provenance records for code and
   creative assets.
