# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-08-29**

## 1. Introduction

CRM SaaS ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

## 2. Data We Collect

### Account Data
- Company name, contact name, email, phone
- Billing information (processed by Stripe/Wompi/PayU - we do not store card numbers)

### Business Data
- Contacts, leads, customers, deals, tickets you create
- Communications (emails, WhatsApp messages, calls)
- Documents and files you upload

### Usage Data
- Feature usage, API calls, login times
- IP address, device type, browser

## 3. How We Use Data

- Provide and improve the Service
- Process payments and manage subscriptions
- Send service notifications
- Prevent fraud and abuse
- Comply with legal obligations

## 4. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your data. We share data only with:
- Payment processors (Stripe, Wompi, PayU, Mercado Pago)
- Communication providers (WhatsApp/Meta, email providers)
- Infrastructure providers (cloud hosting)
- Law enforcement when legally required

## 5. Data Retention

- Active accounts: data retained while subscription is active
- Cancelled accounts: data deleted after 30 days
- Audit logs: retained for 1 year
- Backups: retained for 30 days

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

- Access: request a copy of your data
- Rectification: correct inaccurate data
- Erasure: request data deletion
- Portability: export your data
- Objection: object to certain processing
- Withdraw consent at any time

Contact: privacy@crm-saas.com

## 7. Data Security

- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- Encryption at rest (database)
- Multi-tenant isolation
- Regular security audits
- SOC 2 controls (in progress)

## 8. International Transfers

Data may be processed in: Colombia, USA, EU. We ensure adequate protection levels per GDPR Article 44.

## 9. Cookies

- JWT tokens for authentication (no persistent cookies)
- No tracking cookies
- No advertising cookies

## 10. Children's Privacy

The Service is for businesses only. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

## 11. Changes

We may update this Policy with 30 days notice.

## 12. Contact

Email: privacy@crm-saas.com
